# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Moonshot Academy students are the primary users. Educators and campus staff are secondary users. They use MAC to find rooms, understand whether a space suits a task, see campus activity, and orient themselves in the single documented FF-01 plan.

## Product Purpose

MAC is an interactive campus guide for Moonshot Academy. It connects spaces, environmental readings, events, and navigation to one shared campus map. Success means people can find and understand a real campus space without losing its relationship to the surrounding building.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious, and grounded. The interface can feel playful, while the campus geometry and status information remain legible and honest.

## Anti-references

- Flat colored blocks presented as a faithful architectural model.
- Decorative 3D details presented as measured facts when the drawing does not support them.
- Neon or cyberpunk styling that competes with room and route information.
- Invented floors or connections without source drawings.

## Design Principles

- Keep room identity, environment, events, and navigation attached to the same spatial record.
- Let the source drawing determine spatial relationships; label illustrative geometry clearly.
- Keep the map usable while panels and controls are open.
- Preserve direct manipulation across mouse, trackpad, and touch.
- Use motion to preserve spatial context and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, and reduced motion. Do not use color alone to communicate room status. Support touch pan and pinch without scrolling the page behind the map; keep detail-panel scrolling available.
